📡 Marketing & Advertising Feeds

Your site automatically exposes vehicle feeds for advertising platforms. These feeds are updated in real-time whenever you add, edit, or remove vehicles marked for sale.

🔵 Google Vehicle Ads (JSON)

Feed URL:
https://yourdomain.com/feeds/google-vehicles.json 🔗 View Feed
How to Use:
  1. Sign in to Google Ads
  2. Navigate to Tools & Settings → Business Data → Vehicle Ads
  3. Click + Add Feed
  4. Choose Scheduled Fetch and paste the URL above
  5. Set update frequency (recommended: daily)
  6. Google will automatically fetch your available vehicles and create ads

Note: Only vehicles marked as "For Sale" with status "Available" are included in this feed.

📘 Facebook/Meta Vehicle Catalog (CSV)

Feed URL:
https://yourdomain.com/feeds/meta-vehicles.csv 🔗 View Feed
How to Use:
  1. Go to Facebook Commerce Manager
  2. Select your Business Account or create one
  3. Click Catalog → Add Items
  4. Choose Data Feed as the upload method
  5. Select Scheduled Feed and paste the URL above
  6. Choose CSV format
  7. Set update schedule (recommended: daily at 3 AM)
  8. Connect your catalog to Facebook/Instagram ads

Note: The CSV format follows Facebook's Vehicle Catalog specifications. Only available vehicles for sale are included.

ℹ️ Feed Information

  • Automatic Updates: Feeds update in real-time when you modify vehicle inventory
  • Public Access: These URLs are publicly accessible (no authentication required)
  • Included Vehicles: Only "For Sale" vehicles with "Available" status
  • Data Included: Make, model, year, price, images, mileage, fuel type, transmission, description
  • Image URLs: All image URLs are automatically converted to absolute URLs
  • Performance: Feeds are optimized and cached for fast delivery

🔧 Testing Your Feeds

Before submitting to advertising platforms, test your feeds:

  • Click the "View Feed" buttons above to preview the data
  • Verify that your vehicles appear with correct information
  • Check that images load properly
  • Ensure prices and descriptions are accurate
